Description

Celine is a former demon hunter and a former member of the legendary group Sunlight Sisters, whose voices once helped protect the Honmoon from demonic forces. Known for her strength, discipline, and dedication, she devoted her life to fighting demons and safeguarding humanity.

After the death of Rumi's mother, Celine adopted and raised Rumi as her own daughter. She trained her to become a skilled hunter and later helped prepare HUNTR/X to continue the mission of protecting the Honmoon. As both a mentor and a guardian, Celine played a crucial role in shaping the next generation of demon hunters.

Having witnessed the destruction caused by demons throughout her life, Celine developed a deep hatred and distrust of them. Believing that Rumi's demonic heritage would only bring suffering, she encouraged her to hide and reject that part of herself. Although her actions came from a desire to protect Rumi, they also contributed to Rumi's fear of her own identity.
