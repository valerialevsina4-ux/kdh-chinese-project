Zoey

佐伊

Zuǒyī

Demon Hunter

Character Information

  • Gender: Female

  • Species: Human

  • Group: HUNTR/X

  • Role in Group: Rapper, Maknae

Description

Zoey is a member of Huntrix, a famous girl group whose music helps protect the Honmoon from demonic forces. Energetic, passionate, and determined, she brings enthusiasm and positivity to the team even during the most difficult moments.

Zoey is half Korean and half American, and she sometimes struggles with questions about her identity and where she truly belongs. Because of this, she often worries about how others see her and works hard to fit in. She has a strong desire to make everyone happy and is constantly trying to be the person others need her to be.

As both a performer and a demon hunter, Zoey is always ready to take action. She values honesty, friendship, and teamwork, and she is fiercely protective of the people she cares about. In addition to performing, Zoey enjoys writing song lyrics and expressing her thoughts through music. Before joining Huntrix, she often felt that her work and talents were not truly appreciated. Becoming part of the group gave her a sense of purpose and helped her realize that her contributions matter.

Together with Rumi and Mira, Zoey plays an important role in the battle against Gwi-Ma and the creation of the Golden Honmoon. Her courage, optimism, and loyalty make her an essential member of Huntrix.
