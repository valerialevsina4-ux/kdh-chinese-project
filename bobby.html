Description

Bobby is the manager of HUNTR/X and one of the group's most loyal supporters. Although he is neither a demon hunter nor a fighter, he works tirelessly behind the scenes to help the group succeed and protect their connection with fans.

As Huntrix's manager, Bobby is responsible for organizing schedules, events, performances, and countless daily tasks. His job is demanding, but he approaches it with dedication and enthusiasm. He genuinely cares about Rumi, Mira, and Zoey, treating them not only as artists under his management but also as people he wants to support and protect.

Bobby deeply values the group's fans and understands how important they are to HUNTR/X. His appreciation for their support is evident throughout the story. Rather than focusing only on business matters, he pays attention to the heartfelt messages, handmade signs, and efforts of the fans who believe in the group.
