Healer Han

韩医生

Hán yīshēng

Human

Character Information

  • Gender: Male

  • Species: Human

  • Occupation: Doctor/ Healer

Relationships

  • Rumi: patient.

Description

Healer Han is a practitioner of traditional medicine who runs a small clinic visited by a variety of patients, including celebrities. Proud of his reputation, he decorates the walls of his clinic with photographs of famous visitors, presenting himself as a respected and successful healer.

When Rumi seeks help for the mysterious changes affecting her voice, Healer Han attempts to treat her using traditional remedies and herbal tonics. However, his methods often appear questionable, and he is portrayed more as a well-meaning eccentric than a reliable medical expert. In one memorable example, the "special health tonic" prepared for Rumi turns out to be nothing more than ordinary grape juice.

Although he does not participate in the battles against demons, Healer Han contributes comic relief and highlights the contrast between supernatural problems and ordinary human attempts to solve them.

Chinese Vocabulary

  • 医院 (yīyuàn) - hospital

  • 药 (yào) - medicine

  • 果汁 (guǒzhī) - juice

  • 补药 (bǔyào) - tonic

  • 诊所 (zhěnsuǒ) - clinic
