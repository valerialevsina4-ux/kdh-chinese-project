Description

Derpy is a blue tiger-like demon creature and the loyal companion of Jinu. Known for his oversized glowing eyes, clumsy behavior, and playful personality, he quickly became one of the most recognizable creatures in K-pop Demon Hunters.

Although Derpy is technically a demon, he is far from intimidating. Instead, he is often seen causing harmless trouble, getting into awkward situations, and providing comic relief throughout the story. His curious nature and lovable clumsiness make him a fan favorite.

Derpy is rarely seen alone and is usually accompanied by his closest companion, Sussie the magpie. Together, the two form an inseparable duo and frequently follow Jinu. Despite their mischievous behavior, they remain loyal to their friends and often find themselves involved in important events.

Derpy's design is inspired by Korean folk paintings from the Joseon period known as Hojak-do (호작도), which traditionally depict tigers and magpies together. These paintings often symbolize protection, good fortune, and the relationship between strength and wisdom. Through Derpy and Sussie, the film pays tribute to this important element of Korean cultural heritage.
