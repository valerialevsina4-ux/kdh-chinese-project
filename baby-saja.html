Description

Baby is a member of Saja Boys, a demon group created to help Gwi-Ma weaken the Honmoon and gather human souls. Like the other members, he serves the Demon Lord and participates in the group's mission to increase Gwi-Ma's power.

Despite being a demon, Baby presents himself as the youngest and cutest member of the group. His stage persona is playful, energetic, and childlike, which contrasts with his true nature. As the rapper of Saja Boys, he brings energy and attitude to the group's performances.

Baby is known for his love of spicy food and his mischievous personality. Beneath his cheerful appearance, however, he remains a loyal servant of Gwi-Ma and an active participant in the group's plans against HUNTR/X.
